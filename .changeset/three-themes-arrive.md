Theme system: `init --theme <name>` (with interactive theme picker), new `theme list` and `theme apply <name>` commands, and the active theme recorded in components.json. The registry now serves three OKLCH-scale-based themes (basic, dreamy, utilitarian) at /r/themes/, with W3C DTCG token documents at /r/tokens/. Backwards compatible with registries that only expose the legacy /r/theme.json.
